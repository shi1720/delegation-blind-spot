# Investigator-only consent drafting scaffold

**Not participant-ready. No approval or exemption is claimed. Do not collect consent using this draft.**

Replace and review every bracketed item with the responsible investigator and applicable ethics process. The offline preview collects only acknowledgment that it is a preview.

## Information to provide before a real study

**Study title:** [Final title understood by the intended participant population.]

**Investigator and contact:** [Responsible investigator, affiliation if applicable, monitored email, and independent concern/ethics contact where required.]

**Why you are invited:** [Eligibility and recruitment basis.]

**Purpose:** We are studying how decision support changes observable choices and what those choices reveal about preferences relevant to future product improvements. [Explain any withheld detail and debriefing if formally permitted.]

**What you will do:** [Number of tasks, duration, private preference questions, randomized conditions, follow-up questions, and whether any decision has a real consequence.]

**Recommendation system:** [Precisely identify rule-based, prerecorded model, or live AI assistance. Explain limitations and the participant's ability to ignore it.]

**Information collected:** [Exact responses, timing resolution, identifiers, technical metadata, and any provider transfers. State what is not collected only if verified.]

**Risks and burdens:** [Relevant privacy, time, confusion, and consequential-choice risks and their mitigations. Avoid claiming zero risk.]

**Benefits:** [No guaranteed personal benefit; describe expected research value accurately.]

**Voluntary participation:** [Ability to decline, skip where permitted, stop, and withdraw. Explain consequences and limits once anonymized or published.]

**Compensation:** [Amount, criteria, partial participation, and payment process, or explicitly none.]

**Storage and sharing:** [Storage location, who can access identifiable or coded data, retention, deletion, publication plan, and separate optional public-data consent if needed.]

**Ethics determination:** [Actual body, reference, and contact or documented determination. Leave unresolved until established.]

## Consent mechanism for a future reviewed instrument

Use a clear affirmative action after the participant has received the information and can ask questions. Record the specific consent-document version and date without collecting more identity data than necessary. Keep consent provenance distinct from public analysis data. Never imply that selecting a checkbox proves a response came from a human or establishes independent ethics approval.

If instructions, collection, providers, risks, or sharing change, determine whether participant information and consent must be revised. The investigator is responsible for that decision under the applicable protocol.
