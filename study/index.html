Choosing well. Learning carefully.

A short, offline preview of a delegated-choice study.

Investigator preview only. This is not an approved or active customer study. Exports are permanently labeled preview records and cannot establish human validation.
STEP 1 OF 5 / INFORMATION

Before you explore

You will consider a hypothetical one-night hotel stay, state a preference, choose a hotel, and answer a few short questions. This preview takes about three minutes.

One version provides a simple rule-based recommendation. The other provides no recommendation. Assignment is random. The recommendation is not generated by an AI model.

Your answers remain in this browser tab. Nothing is sent to a server. A downloaded JSON file contains your answers and can be read by anyone you share it with. No name, email, account, or free-text response is requested.

You can stop at any time. Closing or reloading the page clears the session. Downloaded files are yours to delete. The task has no purchase, financial consequence, compensation, or actual booking.

For an actual participant study, the investigator must supply approved study information, contact and withdrawal details, recruitment terms, and an ethics determination. This preview does not collect research consent.

What matters for this stay?

Imagine you are paying for one night before an important meeting. All three hotels have the same price, room quality, cancellation terms, safety, and availability. The differences are quietness and travel time to your meeting.

These answers are recorded separately from the visible hotel choice. In an actual study, the research team would receive them with consent; they are not hidden from the investigator.

Choose a place to stay

Quietness is rated from 0 to 10, with 10 being the quietest. Travel time is the estimated journey to your meeting. Each stay costs 130 study credits.

Now, your own assessment

These responses are reported by you. They are not calculated from the recommendation or your earlier preference.

Your preview is complete

The complete record includes your preference, random assignment, displayed recommendation where applicable, choice, follow-up answers, option order, and elapsed times. The action-only record excludes preference and follow-up answers and demonstrates what a platform observing only the choice might see.

Neither export is evidence from a verified human participant. Do not combine preview records with a future participant dataset.

Offline instrument v0.1.0. No analytics, cookies, network calls, local storage, external fonts, or hidden submission. Hypothetical preferences are not observed purchase behavior.
