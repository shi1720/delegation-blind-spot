Assumptions and provenance

Finite known intent classes, stable class-conditional channels, and the declared sampling design are required. Coordinate confidence boxes account for calibration and field uncertainty. The known synthetic d is treated as fixed. The primary bound has at least 96% marginal coverage under those assumptions, not simultaneous coverage across conditions.

The receipt follow-up was selected after the primary result and reuses original field draws. Its exploratory comparisons have no selective-inference correction or guaranteed post-selection coverage. Equal counts do not equalize API cost, privacy exposure, or elicitation burden. A deterministic parser can extract the supplied leading attribute.
