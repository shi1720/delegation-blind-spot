# Research status

Author: Shivam Gupta. Version 0.3.0. Updated 20 September 2026.

Title: The Delegation Blind Spot: Auditing Product Decisions from Agent Choices.

## Current contribution

A decision-specific audit asks whether a declared logging scheme supports a declared future product comparison, under an explicit calibrated measurement model. It reports compatible value intervals and endpoint witness populations. Established row-space identification, label-shift estimation, measurement-error partial identification and decision-theory results are attributed; this is not a claim to a new general estimator.

The primary experiment made 4,800 actual model requests on synthetic tasks. All 36 conservative decision intervals remain unresolved. A separately frozen, exploratory receipt follow-up made 2,400 requests and resolves 3/9 matched conditions per model by reporting an already supplied preference. A deterministic parser in v0.3 resolves 7/9 on the shared 720 field records, with no new API calls and a known identity channel. This control shows why preserving structured inputs can be preferable to calibrating a model's restatement of them.

The v0.3 controlled simulation contains 14,400 independently sampled multinomial datasets and 43,200 method evaluations. It separates exact structural ambiguity from imprecision under known, calibrated and jointly uncertain channels. All numerical sweep CSV/JSON and parser outputs have been independently reproduced within the workspace. These are internal computational checks, not independent external peer review.

## Submission scope and remaining author responsibilities

The package is prepared for a computational work-in-progress poster. It is not a full-paper contribution proven competitive at a research conference or journal. No manuscript has been uploaded, externally reviewed or accepted. Before upload, the human author must read and take responsibility for the claims, confirm the target venue, complete author and conflict declarations, and check the current portal instructions. See `paper/submission/README.md` for the official call and deadline.

No genuine human participants, customer interviews, deployment outcomes or commercial impact have been observed. Synthetic model roles cannot be relabeled as people. A future human study needs its own recruitment, ethics/consent determination and protocol. The historical investigator-only instrument preview is not collected evidence.

The primary and follow-up model studies were committed before their respective execution; the follow-up was selected after the primary outcome. The new controlled simulation records its pre-execution source and plan hashes but was not externally preregistered. There is no selective-inference correction for exploratory comparisons. Confidence statements are marginal and depend on stable channels, sampling assumptions, the declared class taxonomy and the known synthetic contrast.

For final checks see `docs/release-validation-v03.md`. Prior version history is preserved in Git and the v0.2.0 release.
